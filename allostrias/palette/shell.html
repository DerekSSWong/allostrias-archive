Grim Dawn UI Element Palette

Every interface texture the game ships, decoded and grouped into the elements they actually form. Point at a name here and I will build with that exact texture.

How this was made

Read straight out of resources/UI.arc and the three expansion archives beside it. A .tex is a twelve-byte header over a DDS whose magic is DDSR; icons are uncompressed BGRA, chrome is DXT, and the opaque rules and fills are 24-bit. Nothing here is redrawn or recoloured — the only edit is scaling down to a thumbnail, and every tile names its real pixel size.

Grouping is derived from the filenames, not curated: Crate ships a button as four files ending up/over/down/disabled and a frame as eight ending lt/ct/rt/lm/rm/lb/cb/rb. A suffix only counts when the family it implies exists, so achievementgroup stays one texture rather than becoming a button's rest state.

Art is Crate Entertainment's.
