Where this comes from

Every item, rarity and slot on this page is read from cache/catalogue.sqlite, the single SQLite database allostrias-archive rebuilds from the game's own archives. The stat and skill lines are rendered from each item's .dbr record; the icons are its bitmap= texture, decoded out of Items.arc and packed onto one sheet. The frame, the item tile and the flourishes above are UI.arc textures, unretouched.

Two faces are the game's own — Jura for interface labels and Libertinus Serif, the continuation of the Linux Libertine the game names as linlibertine_r.fnt, for everything an item says. Cinzel stands in for Brioso Pro, which the game licenses and cannot be served here.

Scoped to Legendary and Epic equipment for this prototype. Game art and item text are Crate Entertainment's.
