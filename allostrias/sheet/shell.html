What this is built from

Everything comes out of allostrias-archive: profile.sqlite for the character, its gear and its skill investment, catalogue.sqlite for what those record paths mean, and the archive's own archive/arc.py for the art. Seed replay — the step that turns a record's stored centre into the value an item actually rolled — is the one thing the archive lacked; it is ported in beside the build, not imported, and verified field-by-field against gd-lib as a test oracle. GD Lens is not a dependency of this page and nothing here imports from it.

The page computes the sheet, the build does not. What ships is a contribution list — every stat field with every value feeding it, tagged by source and by the buff that gates it — so the toggles genuinely recompute rather than swapping between stored answers. Hover a stat row, or a worn item, to see what adds up to it.

These rows use formulas checked against a real character sheet: . Every other row is what the records sum to and has not been compared with what the game prints.

Affixes grades every prefix and suffix that can drop against the verdicts on this sheet, your marks included, with every skill in the skill window counted as wanted. A grade says whether an affix's lines are what this build wants — not drop rates, and not the item it lands on. The loot filter it downloads colours each affix by that grade and is meaningful for this character only.
